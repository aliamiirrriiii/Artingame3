How to survive

Keep moving. Standing still is how you die. The ring road around the block is unbroken — when the plaza fills up, start a lap and let them string out behind you.

Points come from damage, not just kills. Chipping a brute still pays. Headshots pay 50% more.

Buy in this order. A real gun by wave 3, Juggernog by wave 6, then the mystery box once you can spare a thousand.

Learn the horde. Runners flank, spitters punish cover, screamers turn the whole street into runners — kill them first. Brutes charge; sidestep, do not backpedal.

Barrels and cars explode. Shoot them when the pack walks past.

Every fifth wave is a boss. The sky turns red. Save a power-up for it.
